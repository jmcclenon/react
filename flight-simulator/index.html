Cockpit Controls

Primary flight controls

  • ↑/W & ↓/S — Elevator (pitch down / up)
  • ←/A & →/D — Ailerons (roll left / right)
  • Q & E — Rudder (yaw left / right)
  • M — Toggle mouse-as-yoke

Engine & configuration

  • Shift / Ctrl — Throttle up / down
  • Z — Full power · X — Idle
  • F / G — Flaps down / up (0–30°)
  • [ / ] — Elevator trim nose up / down

Ground & sim

  • B (hold) / click — Wheel brakes
  • P — Parking brake
  • Space — Pause · R — Reset lesson

The instrument scan (six-pack)

  • ASI airspeed · AI attitude · ALT altitude
  • TC turn coordinator + slip/skid ball
  • HI heading · VSI vertical speed
  • Keep the ball centred — "step on the ball".

A gamepad is supported automatically if connected. Enable Sound for the engine drone and stall-warning horn.
