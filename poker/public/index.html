How to play

  1. Everyone antes. You get one hidden hole card and one up card.
  2. Four betting rounds; a new up card is dealt before each of the last three.
  3. Fold, Check, Call, Bet or Raise on your turn.
  4. Best five-card poker hand at showdown wins the pot.
